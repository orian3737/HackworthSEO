III  ·  Security

Trusted with what matters most

Our clients trust us with sensitive accounts and a reputation they have spent years building. We treat every login, report and file in our keeping as though our own reputation depended on it, because it does.

Your accounts, your keys

Your domain, website, listings and analytics stay in accounts you own. Wherever possible, we work through separate access of our own.

Ahead of the threats

When a new vulnerability surfaces, such as a zero-day in the software your site runs on, we are already watching. We follow current security practice and patch promptly, so you rarely have to face it yourself.

Sensitive data stays out

Analytics are configured so that names, personal details and form contents are never collected or passed to third parties.

Confidential by contract

Every engagement carries a written confidentiality clause covering any non-public information we encounter in the course of our work.

Careful with credentials

We keep sound security practice with every credential in our care, and prefer named, revocable access over shared logins.

A clean departure

If an engagement ends, we remove our access and return every account, setting and record that belongs to you.
