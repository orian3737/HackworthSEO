III  ·  Security

Trusted with what matters most

Our clients hold patient records, privileged matters and controlled work. We treat every login, report and file in our keeping as though our own reputation depended on it, because it does.

Your accounts, your keys

Your domain, website, listings and analytics stay in accounts you own. We work through separate access you grant, never your primary passwords.

Least access, always

We request only the access an engagement requires, and our work never reaches beyond your public website and listings.

Sensitive data stays out

Analytics are configured so that names, health information, case details and form contents are never collected or passed to third parties.

Confidential by contract

Every engagement carries a written confidentiality clause covering any non-public information we encounter in the course of our work.

Careful with credentials

We keep sound security practice with every credential in our care, and prefer named, revocable access over shared logins.

A clean departure

When an engagement ends, we remove our access and return every account, setting and record that belongs to you.

We will never ask you to send patient information, case details or controlled technical data.
